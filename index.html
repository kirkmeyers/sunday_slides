      How Sunday Slides works

      1. Load the requests. The listener on Pro 3 writes YYYYMMDD_DAL_requests.txt into the service folder's Sermon content folder each Sunday morning. Load it with the button, drop it on the page, or open the link on Start Here.

      2. Look over the warnings. Flags from the listener and anything the tool noticed (a reference it adjusted, text that doesn't match the ESV, a verse too long for one slide) are listed on the left and badged on each slide.

      3. Edit. Scripture comes one verse per slide, without verse numbers, with the passage's reference on every slide. In the grid, click a slide to select it, move with the arrow keys, drag to reorder, and double-click or press Enter to open it. In the editor, click the text to type right on the slide, as in ProPresenter. Enter adds a line break, and Option+Enter splits the slide at the cursor. Esc goes back to the grid. Select words and press Dim / undim (or ⌘D) to dim the part of a verse that isn't read. Split, merge, reorder, delete, and add blank slides as needed.

      4. Cues become ProPresenter slide notes on the next slide. Turn a cue into its own text slide or a placeholder from the editor.

      5. Export. ProPresenter bundle makes a .probundle with live, editable text on the theme's own slides. Double-click it on the Pro machine to import. PNGs (transparent, 3840×2160) are there as a backup.

      Review link copies a link with the whole deck in it, for a communicator or producer to check. Proof sheet is a printable list of every slide.
